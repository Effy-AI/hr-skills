# AI usage policy

Copy this file to the root of your Claude-HR folder as `ai-usage-policy.md` and edit it.
Every skill in this plugin reads it before doing anything else.

---

## Naming

- Refer to people by role and initials, never full names. `Hiring manager (A.M.)`, not
  `Andrew Makhovskyi`.
- Job titles and team names may be written in full.

## Data that never enters a document

Do not write any of the following into any file, even if you find it in email, chat, or an
attachment:

- ID or passport numbers
- bank account or payment details
- home addresses
- salary figures or individual compensation
- immigration or visa status
- medical or health information

Describe the step that handles this data instead. "New hire submits bank details in the HRIS"
is correct. The details themselves are not.

## Where sensitive data lives

- Sensitive personal data is submitted directly in the HRIS, never over email or chat.
- If you find sensitive data sitting in an email thread, flag it to me as a process problem.
  Do not copy it anywhere.

## Documents

- Documents link to masters. They do not copy their content.
- Every generated document is a draft until I have reviewed it.

## Uncertainty

- Never state something as fact unless I said it or you can point to evidence.
- Tag uncertain lines and list them at the end so I can close them out.
- Never invent a tool, step, person, or date.
